# v0.4.0 经济基础闭环 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build the first reusable economy foundation: copper-based money, municipal market inventory, stock-driven prices, taxes, basic iron ore, and repair cost rules.

**Architecture:** Keep the MUD engine boundary intact. Shared DTOs expose money/market contracts, `packages/content` defines items/resources/base prices, `packages/game-rules` calculates prices/taxes/repair costs as pure functions, `apps/server` persists market inventory and transactional buy/sell, and `apps/web` renders the market without calculating outcomes locally.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, pnpm workspaces, Fastify, Drizzle/PostgreSQL, React/Vite, Vitest, Playwright.

## Behavioral Rules

- All stored money is integer copper.
- Displayed money is formatted as gold/silver/copper.
- Market purchase requires municipal stock and character money.
- Market sale requires character inventory.
- Player sale adds item quantity to municipal stock.
- Player purchase removes item quantity from municipal stock.
- Every market transaction records gross copper, tax copper, net copper, item, quantity, and direction.
- Tax is a currency sink in v0.4 even if municipal treasury is not yet implemented.
- `iron_ore` must be gatherable from the abandoned iron vein.
- Repair quote must include both copper and iron ore.
- No AI calls or autonomous NPC behavior are introduced in v0.4.
